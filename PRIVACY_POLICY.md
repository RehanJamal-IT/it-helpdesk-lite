# Privacy Policy – IT Help Desk Lite

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

IT Help Desk Lite ("the app") is a free app made by **Rehan Jamal**. It helps employees send IT support requests to their company's IT team.

## Summary
**The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.** It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app stores
The details you type in (name, department, mobile number, work e-mail, office) and the IT team's e-mail addresses are saved **only on your own device**, so you don't have to type them again. They never leave your device unless you send a ticket.

## How a ticket is sent
When you press **Send to IT**, the app opens **your own e-mail app** (for example Gmail or Outlook) with a ready message. The message contains your details, your problem description, basic device information (phone maker, model and Android version) and any photos you chose. **You** decide whether to press Send. The message goes only to the e-mail addresses your IT team set in the app. The app developer never receives it.

## Photos and files
Photos are attached only when you choose them. The app does not access any other photos or files.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the app, or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → IT Help Desk Lite → Storage → Clear storage). This removes everything the app saved.

## Children
The app is made for workplaces and is not directed at children.
