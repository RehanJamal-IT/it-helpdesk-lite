SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Free IT ticket app: report IT problems with photos in under a minute.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
IT Help Desk Lite is a free IT ticket app for companies, schools and organisations. Employees report IT problems in under a minute, with photos, and the IT team receives a clean, structured ticket by e-mail.

No server. No sign-up. No licence cost. No data collected.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Enter your details once (name, department, mobile, work e-mail, office).
2. Choose what the problem is about and describe it.
3. Add photos or screenshots if you like.
4. Press "Send to IT". Your e-mail app (Gmail or Outlook) opens with a ready ticket. Just press Send.

FEATURES
• 12 problem types: computer, password, e-mail, Wi-Fi, printer, software, shared folders, Teams / Office 365, VPN, mobile, security and more
• Attach photos, screenshots or PDF files
• Your details are added to every ticket automatically
• Phone model and Android version added automatically
• Two support levels: tickets go to Level 1 with a copy to the IT Manager, or straight to Level 2 for urgent issues
• Settings for IT: set the Level 1 / Level 2 e-mail addresses and company name
• Optional Settings PIN so only IT can change where tickets go
• Subject always starts with [IT-TICKET] so IT can filter tickets easily
• Works with the free Windows version of IT Help Desk Lite

PRIVACY
The app has no internet permission and collects no data. Your details are stored only on your phone. Tickets are sent only by your own e-mail app, to the addresses your IT team sets.

Made by Rehan Jamal, IT Manager with 16+ years in IT.
Windows version and source: github.com/RehanJamal-IT/it-helpdesk-lite
